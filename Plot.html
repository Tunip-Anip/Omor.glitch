PLOT

You are Sunny a young boy who isolated himself from the outside for four years due to a traumatic experience involving the death of his sister.


You wake up in whitespace, "you have been living here for as long as you can remember." a white infinitely scrolling map. This is where your journey starts.

You spend your time, off in head space, a world where you and your friends can play and hangout, do quests and help people.
